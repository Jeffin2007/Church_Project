## Image Requirements

### 1. Hero Section (`/hero/`)

**Required Images:**

- `church-altar.jpg` - Main hero background (1920x1080 or higher)
  - **Content:** Beautiful shot of the church altar, sanctuary, or interior
  - **Style:** Well-lit, sacred atmosphere, can be slightly wide angle
  - **Use:** Full-screen cinematic background with slow zoom animation

---

### 2. Church Images (`/church/`)

**Required Images:**

- `exterior.jpg` - Church building exterior (1600x1200)
  - **Content:** Front view of Queen of All Saints Church building
  - **Style:** Daytime, clear sky, welcoming perspective
  - **Use:** Welcome Section

## Image Specifications

### Technical Requirements:

- **Format:** JPG or WebP (WebP preferred for performance)
- **Quality:** High quality, minimum 80% JPEG quality
- **Color Space:** sRGB
- **Optimization:** Compress for web while maintaining quality

### Style Guidelines:

- **Lighting:** Well-lit, avoid dark or underexposed images
- **Composition:** Follow rule of thirds, balanced framing
- **Sacred Atmosphere:** Images should convey reverence and community
- **Avoid:** Flash photography harsh shadows, blurry images
